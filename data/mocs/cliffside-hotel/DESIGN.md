# Cliffside Hotel - approved direction

The user approved `concept-approved.png` on 2026-09-11 after requesting an
original architectural model around 2,000+ pieces. All owned sets may donate.
The image is AI concept art, not a measured or verified model. It was generated
with the built-in image tool as a Mediterranean cliffside hotel with layered
ivory buildings, warm roofs, balconies, a pool terrace and winding stone stairs.

## Design intent

- Target about 2,500 pieces; 2,200-2,800 is a working range, not a certified BOM.
- Target 48 x 40 studs, with a taller three-storey main building toward the rear
  left, a two-storey guest wing to the right, and a lower connecting terrace.
- Keep warm white/tan walls, recessed openings, balconies, dark restrained
  railings, brown timber details, warm roofing and a pale-blue pool.
- Preserve the stepped silhouette, courtyard, broad stairs and integrated
  cliff. Simplify the dense rockwork and roof ornament shown in the concept.
- Use accessible furnished rooms where the piece allowance permits. Separate
  assemblies and roofs should support construction and inspection.

## Constraints and verification

Use exact element IDs from the tracked collection. Until better source evidence
is established, count one exact ID per owned donor-set copy, deduplicating rows
within each set. Do not consume the previous cafe's BOM as a reservation: all
sets remain available and no physical model has been recorded as built.

Any actual per-set quantities added to this rule require a cited source and
visual/text verification; absence from another set must not be inferred.
The new model must not silently inherit v1's incorrect rectangular-part axes.
Regular LDraw parts run along local X, and accessory origins use the actual
mounting body bottoms. Use canonical Studio shape/color mapping, with geometry
provenance. Avoid changing installed applications; use the working CPU renderer.

Exports must distinguish inventory validation, geometric bounds, collision
checks, attachment continuity and physical build testing. A physical build
cannot be certified by the digital authoring process.

## First critical prototype

Resolve the warm roof palette and repeated facade openings before generating
the full model. Inventory minima for matching roof slopes are low. A measured
roof module or source-backed donor quantities must establish feasibility before
the image's roof treatment is promised.
