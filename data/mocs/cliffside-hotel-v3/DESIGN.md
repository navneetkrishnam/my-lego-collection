# Refined coastal composition

User direction: prioritize resemblance to the approved Cliffside Hotel
concept, with a maximum of 3,500 pieces. All owned sets are donor candidates.

- Preserve the revised main facade and three-building height hierarchy.
- Replace every hinged roof with ordinary stud-connected construction.
- Reduce the main roof's visible tier height and maintain brown exposed edges.
- Raise the cliff and introduce a turning stair route and arched landing.
- Use hollow terrain where practical, while explicitly supporting loose ledges.
- Add actual owned rock and plant elements without inventing quantities.
- Check front and rear actual-part renders, stock, connectivity and geometry.

The resulting CAD draft is 3,275 pieces. Keep the prior 2,648-piece package for
comparison. Final building instructions await visual review and trial assembly.
