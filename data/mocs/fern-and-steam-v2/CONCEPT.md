# Fern & Steam / Courtyard Edition

Status: aesthetic concept for review, generated with the built-in image tool.
`concept.png` is concept art, not a render of a verified LEGO model. No BOM,

## Direction

A compact conservatory cafe with arched openings, recessed windows, a pitched
glass canopy, warm white/tan masonry, restrained forest-green accents, brown
woodwork and an entirely composed tiled terrace. Raised planting, a vine,
an integrated bench and small coffee details make it feel inhabited.

The original block-like silhouette, broad exposed foundation and sparse
planting were not aesthetically successful. This concept explores stronger
proportions, texture, depth and a coherent palette before detailed modeling.
