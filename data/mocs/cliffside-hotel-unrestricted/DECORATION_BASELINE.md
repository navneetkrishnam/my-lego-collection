# Hotel decoration baseline

Use [the user-supplied Cliffside Hotel concept](reference-concept.png) as the visual baseline for all exterior decoration reviews. The user reaffirmed this reference after reviewing garden/pool v3.

The approved v6 floor program and building positions remain the architectural baseline. Judge decoration against this concept while preserving that later layout.

- Distribute terracotta pots around entrances, gardens, balconies, landings and pool/service terraces. Vary pot size, plant form and grouping; keep usable routes clear.
- Build the cliff from connected fractured masses, irregular ledges and smaller fallen rocks. Avoid upright columns on a grid or a repeated block pattern.
- Place greenery where it can grow from a pot, bed, rock crevice or supported planter. Concentrate planting in selected pockets and leave rock and masonry visible.
- Creepers need unequal lengths, asymmetrical branches, patchy density and identifiable roots. Avoid mirrored ribbons and repeated vertical strips.
- Retain the cream stone, terracotta roofs, green shutters, flowering pergolas and warm lantern character.
- Review the whole-model daylight image against the reference, then inspect the front, overhead, garden, pool and dusk views. Check modeled circulation and artifact provenance separately from visual quality.

The current authored model is still an architectural proxy. Visual resemblance does not establish LEGO part availability, legal connections, physical stability or assembly feasibility.
