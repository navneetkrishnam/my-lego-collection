# Comparison with the approved concept

| Feature | Approved image | Current actual-part revision |
|---|---|---|
| Overall composition | Three-storey hotel, lower connecting volume, two-storey wing | Same height hierarchy, with a wider main facade |
| Main windows | Deep arches with substantial piers | Genuine 15254 semicircular arches; four-stud openings, three-stud piers |
| Balconies | Thin dark rails with varied placement | Black handle-tile rails; asymmetrical main balconies and a wing balcony |
| Shutters | Dark green, finely ribbed | Green vertically mounted four-stud plates |
| Roofs | Curved terracotta tiles and hip corners | Stepped slope roof on the main building; hinged plate roofs on the wings |
| Courtyard | Dense furniture, greenery and several levels | Pergola, tables, chairs, small planters and pool terrace; sparser layout |
| Cliff | Tall, irregular rock face with layered masonry and winding stairs | Lower stepped rock masses, straight broad staircase, simpler retaining walls |
| Palette | Consistent warm cream, terracotta and dark green | Tan/white main building, white wings, brown roofs, several green shades |
| Build evidence | AI concept; no verified parts list | Actual CAD placements, donor allocation and scoped digital checks |

The new render improves the facade's masonry-to-window ratio and gives each
building a distinct architectural role. It still does not reproduce the
reference's fine roof texture, cliff height, winding circulation or density of
planting. Passing digital checks establishes neither visual equivalence nor
physical buildability. Those remain separate review decisions.
