# Cliffside Hotel revision — return to the approved architecture

The user rejected the first completed draft because it did not resemble the
approved concept. This revision keeps that concept as the visual brief.

The first deliverable is an actual-brick main-building study: 24 studs wide,
14 deep, three seven-brick storeys. Three four-stud clear openings alternate
with three-stud piers and margins. Recessed glazing and six-stud semicircular
arches establish masonry depth. The wall palette is consistent tan with white
arch surrounds and restrained cornices. Thin black balcony rails replace the
heavy white balustrades; greenery and shutters sit beside openings.

A continuous warm stepped roof replaces the detached-looking panels. This
study precedes the complete terrace/wing/cliff design. It must read as a
Mediterranean masonry building at thumbnail size before expansion.

Use all donor sets, exact elements and conservative/source-backed counts.
Do not treat the 2,188-piece rejected version as a visual baseline or pad a
piece target with repetitive rock columns. Keep previous files as history.
Physical strength is untested; digital allocation and geometry are separate
from aesthetic acceptance.
