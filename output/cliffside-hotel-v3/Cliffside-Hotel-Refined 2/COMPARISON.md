shutters and asymmetrical black balconies. The higher cliff and turning stairs
address the largest proportional differences in the previous actual render.

The reference remains richer in curved roof tiles, fine masonry and climbing
vegetation. The current model is an actual-part architectural interpretation,
not an exact reconstruction of the AI image. Physical buildability and the
user's aesthetic acceptance are separate from the digital checks.
